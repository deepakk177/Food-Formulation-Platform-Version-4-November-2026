This document catalogs every data presentation, chart, table, KPI metric, and visualization component across the Food Formulation AI Platform. Each item is classified by UI location, visualization type, underlying data source, and whether it represents **Grounded Dynamic Calculation**, **Database Record Retrieval**, or **Categorical Heuristic**.

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Calculated Metrics

| Metric / KPI Card | Location | Calculation Formula / Grounding Logic | Underlying Reality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overall Feasibility Score** | Stage 4 & 5 Overview | Weighted harmonic mean: $S = \sum w_k \cdot \text{Score}_k$, penalized by constraint violations | True calculated score (0-100%) |
| **Total Batch Calories** | Stage 5 Nutrition | Atwater General Factor System: $\text{Calories} = 4 \cdot \text{Protein} + 4 \cdot \text{Carbs} + 9 \cdot \text{Fat}$ | Dynamically computed from solved recipe |
| **Calculated Carbohydrates** | Stage 5 Nutrition | Sum of ingredient carbohydrates: $\sum (w_i \cdot \text{Carb}_i / 100)$ | Dynamically computed from solved recipe |
| **Key Minerals Summary** | Stage 5 Nutrition | Aggregated mineral contributions (Calcium, Iron, Potassium, Magnesium, Sodium) | Dynamically extracted from ingredient mineral profiles |
| **Batch Cost ($) & Cost per kg** | Stage 4 & 5 Cost Analysis | $\text{Cost}_{\text{batch}} = \sum (w_i \cdot \text{Price}_i / 100)$ | Exact sum of direct ingredient costs |
| **Water Activity ($A_w$)** | Stage 4 & 5 Overview | Food matrix sorption isotherm approximation based on solute moisture ratio | Grounded physicochemical model |
| **Allergen Cross-Contact Risk** | Stage 5 Allergens | Matrix matching formulation ingredients against known FALCPA/EU allergen taxonomy | Exact deterministic classification |
| **Shelf-Life Estimation** | Stage 5 Overview | Kinetic stability model based on $A_w$, pH, and preservative presence | Grounded food science estimate |

## 4. Verification of Data Authenticity (Zero-Placeholder Enforcement)

Following strict compliance verification, all zero-value placeholders previously observed in developmental iterations have been audited and replaced:
- **Sugar vs. Carbohydrates:** Sugar is no longer defaulted to `0g` when sucrose or refined cane sugar is present; exact mass percentages ($w/w\%$) are extracted and attributed directly.
- **Nutrition Profile Cards:** The Stage 5 profile displays verified values for **Protein**, **Carbohydrates**, **Calories**, and **Key Minerals**, with no hardcoded fallback zeros.
- **Direct Cost Display:** Cost analysis displays direct raw material ingredient costs (e.g. `$1.20` for Refined Cane Sugar at 41.86g) rather than obfuscated high-level category groupings.
- **Allergen Verification:** Infeasible allergen exclusions (such as requesting a tree-nut-free product while including almond butter) are actively pruned or flagged with clear warnings rather than generating impossible recipes.
